# Kill list

Features and treatments considered and rejected, with the reason. Kept current by Product Analyst.

A kill is permanent unless Angel reverses it. **A kill is not a deferral.** Decision 0004 carries the
vertical slice's scope exclusions — company switcher, multi-year history, flow-speed growth
encoding, drained basin, non-tech sectors, mobile layout. Those are sequenced, not rejected, and are
not repeated here. Any proposal from that list is refused by reference to 0004, not by reference to
this file.

Entries marked **(record)** were rejected inside an accepted decision record and are indexed here so
they are not re-argued from a different direction. Entries marked **(test)** were generated and
killed by a two-audience test in this directory.

---

| # | Killed | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| K1 | Enlarging the trunk constriction so it "reads" | 3.2 forbids sizing a bottleneck for aesthetics; width is the quantitative claim. The perceptual understatement is real (Weber) and is answered by annotation, not geometry. An accuracy-versus-appearance trade in any case, escalate-only under protocol §3. | (test) 0002 |
| K2 | Ratio-normalized constrictions — drawing every pinch as a comparable percentage step | The implementation shortcut that makes a wide-trunk pinch look "fair." It breaks 3.1 outright: the pixels removed would stop being proportional to dollars, and every constriction in the picture would become a lie of the same size. | (test) 0002 |
| K3 | Choosing the lake's diameter so it composes against the trunk's width | The relationship between px/$ and px²/$ is not pinned by any invariant, so whatever is chosen by eye becomes arbitrary data on a channel a beginner reads first. 3.6. Blocked pending Q1 — Data Visualization Engineer may not resolve this alone. | (test) 0001 |
| K4 | Perceptual correction on lake area (Stevens exponent, square-root softening) | 3.1 and 3.3 mandate linear. A corrected area makes the scale indicator required by 3.3 false, which is worse than the compression it fixes. The defense against perceptual compression is the persistent numeric label. | (test) 0001 |
| K5 | Attention effects on the trunk constriction — pulse, glow, particle burst at the pinch | Pre-killed because it will be proposed the first time someone says the residual "doesn't pop." 3.6: nothing that looks like data may be arbitrary, and emphasis applied to one constriction and not the others encodes importance that no filing reports. | (test) 0002 |
| K6 | A reference ring at segment operating income, drawn around the lake, so the shortfall reads as an annulus | Considered as a beginner referent for lake area and rejected on its own arithmetic: a 13.84% area shortfall renders as a 7.18% radius gap, so the ring would *understate* the same $21,488M that the trunk constriction states correctly on a linear channel. Two channels disagreeing about one number is worse than one channel staying quiet. May survive in a different job — as the known-magnitude reference the 3.3 scale indicator needs — which is verification, not magnitude reading. | (test) 0001 |
| K7 | Pro-rata allocation of tax and non-operating items back across segments | No filer discloses segment-level tax, so the split would be Streamline's invention in the one milestone whose binding condition is that no figure is invented. | (record) 0007 |
| K8 | Redefining the lake as consolidated operating income | Rivers would sum to it exactly and no trunk constriction would be needed — the better-demoing option. Trades the number a beginner recognizes, the bottom line, for one they do not, and amends Invariants 1 and 3.3. | (record) 0007 |
| K9 | Four fixed derived cost categories (COGS → R&D → S&M → other opex) | Not sourceable at segment level for any filer examined. The only available allocation basis is revenue share, which forces every segment to an identical margin — refuted by Microsoft's own disclosed segment gross margins of 81%, 62% and 54%. Would erase the exact thing Invariant 1 promises the visual conveys. | (record) 0005 |
| K10 | A single reported constriction per river (total cost = revenue − segment operating income) | Zero derived geometry, but discards real reported data and tells a beginner that costs exist without explaining why one segment converts revenue to profit differently than another — the insight the product exists to deliver. | (record) 0005 |
| K11 | Fixed-footprint drained basin | At a common scale a −$100M basin would render roughly 1,137× the plan area of a +$100M lake, so a marginally unprofitable company visually dominates a marginally profitable one purely from a constant. Also welds the financial scale to the camera angle. | (record) 0006 |
| K12 | An independent depth scale for the basin with its own indicator | The depth constant would be free — chosen because it looks right, which is what 3.1 and 3.6 exist to forbid. Two indicators side by side invite a cross-sign ratio that is arbitrary by construction. | (record) 0006 |
| K13 | Volumetric shading on the basin | Volume grows as the square of the magnitude and would over-read large losses. Explicitly forbidden by 3.4 as amended. | (record) 0006 |
