# 0033 — Visual direction feedback: vertical, and the water reads too flat

Date:        2026-08-23
Status:      recorded, unbuilt
From:        Angel

ORIENTATION
-----------

Angel envisioned the picture VERTICAL — the lake at the TOP with the streams flowing up into it
from the BOTTOM. The current build is horizontal, left to right. He wants the whole picture
visible in one glance without scrolling.

LOOK
----

Angel's assessment of the current render is that it "looks horrible" aesthetically and "doesn't
look like a river at all" — he expected something more vibrant, water that reads as actual water
and an actual lake, rather than a diagram of the shape of one.

Honest cause worth recording: DESIGN.md derived the water palette from pixel measurements of real
water photography (2–10% saturation, dark median), which is technically defensible and visually
inert. The tension is between accurate water and evocative water, and the brief chose accurate.

He is explicit that this is NOT a priority now — numbers first, interface later — and that the
framework, streams and lake are "a good idea" and "a good starting point." Project horizon he
stated: six to eight months.

Consequence: Nothing built. Recorded so the direction is not lost and the cause is not
             rediscovered. This is a design constraint for when the visual work is eventually
             prioritized. The orientation and aesthetic direction both require Art Director and
             Product Analyst involvement once the slice numbers are stable.
